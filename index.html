🗑️ Delete Your Account

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the BeeWell app
  2. Go to your Profile
  3. Navigate to Privacy & Security
  4. Select “Delete Account”
  5. Confirm your request

What Happens

  • Your account data is permanently deleted
  • You lose access immediately

Data That May Remain

  • Some posts/messages may remain in the system
  • They are no longer linked to your account
